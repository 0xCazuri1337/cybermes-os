 [MS-WCCE]: Windows Client Certificate Enrollment Protocol

 In this article
 
 
 Specifies the Windows Client Certificate Enrollment
Protocol, which consists of a set of DCOM interfaces that enable clients to
request various services from a certification authority (CA). These services
enable X.509 (as specified in [X509]) digital certificate enrollment, issuance,
revocation, and property retrieval.

updated frequently. We recommend you subscribe to the RSS
