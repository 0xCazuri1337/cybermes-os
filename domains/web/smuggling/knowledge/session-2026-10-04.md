### `smuggling.lead.bbd_ctbb.show` — HTTP/2 framing and WAF desync
Claim as filed: *"Public writeup evidence for smuggling: HTTP/2 framing WAF
bypass."*

Corroborated by an independent second publisher, so it promoted at **0.82**.

**Read the claim as a retrieval anchor, not as knowledge.** This item was
produced by the aggregator harvest (`bugbountydaily` RSS → linked article), so
its claim is a topic label rather than a mechanism statement. The mechanism, the
disproof clause and both verbatim quotes are in the item body and in
`DIGEST.md`; the `HUNT.md` runbook maps it to the `filter_bypass` procedure.

**What the mechanism is, in short** (from the cited article, not invented here):
HTTP/2's multi-frame architecture lets an attacker split a request across frames
so that a reverse proxy inspecting one frame and an origin parsing the reassembled
stream disagree about request boundaries — timing delays, protocol-translation
flaws, and incomplete body inspection between hops each contribute.

**Operational consequence for a hunt.** If the front end is HTTP/1.1-only and the
origin speaks HTTP/2, or a CDN terminates and re-frames, then every front-end
"single request" assumption is untested. Confirm content routing to a
back-end-only host before drawing any conclusion from a 200 or a 4xx.
